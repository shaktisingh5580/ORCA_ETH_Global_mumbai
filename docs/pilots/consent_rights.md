# P13 pilot consent and data rights

Start collection only after the responsible project owner records approval and each participant voluntarily agrees to the study scope. Use participant and custodian codes in replay inputs. Keep any identity mapping and signed consent in the institution's restricted records.

- Record the research task, workflows involved, what observations will be collected, how they will be used and who can access them. Record an agreed retention period and a contact for withdrawal or corrections.
- Confirm the data owner and the approved rights for each exact input package, including tool/model processing and any provider transfer. Keep the data and timing logs in the approved environment.
- Keep reference values and approval records with the separate custodian until outputs are sealed. The replay validates digests and separation of recorded identities. The study owner verifies independently that they represent separate people or teams and that the held-out data was unavailable to workflow operators.
- Review distribution rights before releasing results, screenshots, research files, or reports. Use only approved aggregate metrics in the redacted report. Remove identities, private coordinates, raw artifacts and confidential claims.
- Record participation and exclusions from the actual study. A rehearsal, volunteer involvement, or technical evaluation provides no adoption evidence by itself.
